PIR — Physics Intermediate Representation

The equation was already in the data. PIR reads it out.

A classical engine that recovers closed-form physical laws from noisy numerical data — log-linearization, dimensional consistency scoring, and sparse regression. No black box, no learned weights standing in for an answer: just the equation, in symbols you can check by hand.

How it works

Four classical stages, no learned priors.

Log-linearize

Any monomial law y = C·x₁^a₁·x₂^a₂··· becomes exactly linear once you take logs. Ordinary least squares recovers the exponents directly — no search required for this large class of physical laws.

Score against dimensions

Candidate exponents are checked against known physical units with a soft dimensional penalty, so structurally implausible fits are down-weighted rather than accepted on curve-fit quality alone.

RANSAC + sparse regression

Robust fitting handles outliers and sensor noise; sparse regression keeps the recovered expression as short as the data actually supports.

Residual refinement

Remaining structure in the residual is re-tested against the same pipeline, letting compound laws be recovered in stages rather than requiring one search to find everything at once.
